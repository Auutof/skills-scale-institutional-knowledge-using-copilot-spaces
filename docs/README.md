# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

OctoAcme manages cross-functional projects through a customer-focused, iterative lifecycle. Teams validate the problem and expected outcomes, plan scope and a prioritized backlog, deliver in small increments, and use evidence to adjust as they learn. Clear ownership and psychological safety support collaboration and continuous learning.

Project Managers coordinate schedules, risks, dependencies, and communication; Product Managers define outcomes, prioritize the roadmap, and measure impact; developers design, build, and test the solution. QA and stakeholders help validate acceptance criteria and provide input or approvals. The team uses project boards, backlogs, milestones, and a risk register to make work and decisions visible.

During execution, teams track progress through standups, delivery syncs, demos, and stakeholder updates. Work is reviewed against acceptance criteria and a Definition of Done, with automated tests, linting, security scans, and appropriate manual or end-to-end testing supporting quality. Risks and blockers are reviewed regularly and escalated from the team to the Project Manager, Product Lead, and sponsor as needed.

Before release, teams confirm acceptance criteria, CI and security checks, release notes, smoke tests, and rollback plans. They deploy and verify the release, communicate with stakeholders, then hold retrospectives after sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents. Retrospective actions are assigned owners and due dates and tracked so lessons lead to measurable, continuous improvement.

## Project Lifecycle Overview

1. **Initiate:** Validate the problem, business need, stakeholders, success measures, and whether to proceed.
2. **Plan:** Agree on scope, backlog, estimates, Definition of Done, dependencies, milestones, and QA approach.
3. **Execute and track:** Build, test, review, demonstrate progress, and manage risks and blockers.
4. **Release:** Meet release checks, deploy, verify production behavior, and communicate the outcome.
5. **Improve:** Capture learnings in a retrospective and track follow-up actions.
